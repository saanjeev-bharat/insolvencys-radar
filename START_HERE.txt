INSOLVENCY RADAR - FREE ONLINE SETUP (about 15 minutes, no payment, no card)

You need: an email address. That is all.

STEP 1. Make a free GitHub account
  - Go to github.com and click Sign up. Verify your email.

STEP 2. Make a new project
  - Click the + at the top right, then "New repository".
  - Name: insolvency-radar
  - Choose "Public". (Free sites need Public.)
  - Click "Create repository".

STEP 3. Upload the files
  - Unzip insolvency_radar.zip on your computer.
  - On the new repository page click "uploading an existing file".
  - Open the unzipped folder and select EVERYTHING inside it: the folders
    docs, radar, data, tests and the files requirements.txt, README.md.
    Drag them all into the browser window. Wait until the upload finishes.
  - Click "Commit changes".

STEP 4. Add the daily job file (one small extra step)
  - Click "Add file" then "Create new file".
  - In the name box type exactly:  .github/workflows/update.yml
    (typing the slashes makes the folders)
  - Open the file "update.yml.txt" from the unzipped folder in Notepad,
    copy everything, and paste it into the big box on GitHub.
  - Click "Commit changes", then "Commit changes" again.

STEP 5. Turn on the free website
  - Click "Settings" (top row), then "Pages" in the left menu.
  - Under "Branch" choose "main", and the folder "/docs". Click Save.
  - Wait 2 minutes, refresh the page. Your web address appears at the top,
    like  https://YOURNAME.github.io/insolvency-radar/

STEP 6. Fetch real data now (do not wait for tomorrow)
  - Click "Actions" (top row). If asked, click "I understand, enable".
  - Click "Update data" on the left, then "Run workflow", then the green button.
  - Wait about 2 minutes until you see a green tick.
  - Open your web address again. Real notices appear if the collector worked.

AFTER THAT
  - It updates itself every day. You do nothing.
  - On your phone, open the address and choose "Add to Home screen" to get an app icon.

IF IT GOES WRONG
  - A red cross in Actions means the collector failed. Click it, copy the red
    error text, and send it to me. The first run often needs a small fix
    because the collector has never been tested against the real ASIC site.
