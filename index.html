Insolvencys Radar

Australian companies in liquidation, administration, receivership or strike-off, from ASIC's open data. Each card names the appointed practitioner where ASIC lists one.

    Free public search

    Sources: ASIC insolvency statistics (Series 1 and 2), ASIC Company Dataset and ASIC Liquidator Dataset, published by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission. The two datasets on data.gov.au are licensed CC BY 3.0 AU.

    ASIC publishes these weekly and the appointment records run about two weeks behind, so recent events may be missing. ASIC's data names the appointee but gives no phone or email. A firm name appears only when the appointee matches one registered liquidator exactly. Always confirm the appointment in ASIC's published notice before acting.
